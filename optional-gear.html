Recommended Yet Not Required

There are only 4 things here, and those are a Snark Tuner, a guitar strap, a guitar stand, and a variety of "picks" or "plectrums" depending on where you are from.

With a clip-on tuner you can get right in tune to start playing, which I would recommend doing every time you pick up your guitar, as it's a good habit to get into. The Snark Tuner is very easy to use and quite cheap. The only reason it is here and not in the normal gear page is because you could do it by ear or with an app on your phone.

As for the guitar strap, if you ever plan on going out and performing, you might not always have the opportunity to sit down and play like you do at home. You go to a concert, the members of the band are all standing, and what do they have? A strap. It would be way too much work to try and hold the guitar up all on your own and play it by yourself, but hey, if you can somehow manage it, you'll be legendary for sure.

What about the guitar stand? Well, playing and learning guitar is an absolute ton of fun but as some point, you might get a little worn out and want to put it down. You wouldn't want to leave it somewhere it might get stepped on or knocked over, and you wouldn't want to put it in some closet and possibly forget, display that bad boy! having a guitar nearby inspires you to play more, and in this journey, inspiration is the name of the game.

Next are picks. The reason this is optional is that many people use their fingers to play. This is called fingerstyle. While this technique is fun and worthwhile to learn, I wouldn't recommend it if you are just starting out.
